  1. 24.6%of annual shrinkage recovered, ≈ €55.5k a year
    Year 2 · eLab Business Case II · team of 6

    Catching what self-checkout misses

    Unsupervised fraud detection for a supermarket chain with no fraud labels. Over 100,000 shopping sessions were turned into behavioural features such as scan speed, movement through the store and basket value. They were then scored by an ensemble of 27 Isolation Forests, refined with LOF, PCA and K-Means shopper profiles. The top 150 sessions go to staff for spot-checks.

  2. −41%annual network cost, over €1M saved a year
    Year 2 · eLab Business Case II · team of 6

    Optimising a parcel network's last mile

    Which of Maastricht's 35 parcel service points should stay, move or close, and where should automated lockers go? The project combines a demand model, a pickup-behaviour regression, road distances from Dijkstra, a p-median MILP and simulated annealing. The recommended network has 11 service points and 4 lockers, keeps a 63.8% pickup rate and held up under sensitivity tests.
